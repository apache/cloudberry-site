Apache Cloudberry (Incubating) has several public accounts on third-party
platforms. They help the community share news and stay in touch. This page
lists the official accounts and the people who manage them, so you can tell
which accounts are real.

All of these accounts belong to the project. They are run on behalf of the
community by members of the Podling Project Management Committee (PPMC) and
by volunteer committers. They are not owned by any person or company.

:::info
These are extra channels. The project's main channels are the [mailing
lists](/community/mailing-lists) and [GitHub](https://github.com/apache/cloudberry).
Project decisions are made on the mailing lists.
:::

:::note About `cloudberrydb@gmail.com`
This Google account was created before Cloudberry joined the Apache
Incubator, so it still uses the old `cloudberrydb` name. The address can't
be renamed, and several accounts above (X, YouTube, and the WeChat Official
Account) are registered with it, so the project keeps using it. It is an
official project account, and it also hosts the public community calendar.

Anyone who can sign in to this Google account can also sign in to the
accounts registered with it, such as X.
:::

## Help manage an account

Any PPMC member or committer can help manage these accounts. If you want to
help, send an email to
[dev@cloudberry.apache.org](mailto:dev@cloudberry.apache.org) and say which
account you want to help with. One of the current managers will add you and
update this page.

Login details are never shared in public. They are passed only privately
between account managers.

## Report a fake account

If you find an account that claims to represent Apache Cloudberry but is
not listed on this page, please tell us at
[dev@cloudberry.apache.org](mailto:dev@cloudberry.apache.org) or
[private@cloudberry.apache.org](mailto:private@cloudberry.apache.org).
