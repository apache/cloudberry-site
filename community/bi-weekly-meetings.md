# Bi-weekly Meetings

Every two weeks, contributors and users of Apache Cloudberry meet online to
share progress, discuss proposals, and plan what comes next. Everyone is
welcome to join!

## When

- Friday, 16:00-16:45 UTC+8 / 11:00-11:45 UTC+3 / 08:00-08:45 UTC+0 / 00:00-00:45 UTC-8, every two weeks
- [Community Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=cloudberrydb%40gmail.com&ctz=UTC)
  ([iCal](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/cloudberrydb%40gmail.com/public/basic.ics))
- The meeting may be skipped or rescheduled during global or regional
  holidays. Please check the calendar for the latest schedule.

## Join

- The meeting is held on Google Meet. You can find the link in the calendar
  event and the meeting minutes.
- Add your agenda items and your name to the attendee list in the
  [meeting minutes][minutes].
- Follow-up discussions happen on the [dev mailing list](/community/mailing-lists).
